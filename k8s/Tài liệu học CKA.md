# Free materials for CKA – Certified Kubernetes Administrator exam

As a result of great interest in my previous post on free CKAD exam materials, I decided to prepare another article on the next exam. This time I have prepared an list containing free study materials preparing for CKA - Certified #Kubernetes Administrator.

I have described all the materials in detail on my blog at:  [https://www.czerniga.it/2022/01/16/free-study-materials-for-cka-certified-kubernetes-administrator-exam/](https://www.czerniga.it/2022/01/16/free-study-materials-for-cka-certified-kubernetes-administrator-exam/)

Let me know if you are interested in other exams for which I can prepare such lists.
